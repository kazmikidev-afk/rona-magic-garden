# Graphics round 2
Local stylized WebGL 3D in home, slime, wardrobe and zoo. Free Three.js (MIT license bundled), original procedural geometry and materials, lighting, shadows and drag rotation. Not photorealism. No activity trimming in this phase.

Round 2 adds fox tail and ear motion, bird hops, breathing, doll turns, slime mixing wobble, visible toppings, colour-matched hairstyle extensions, larger headband and care-progress props. Group resources are disposed on replacement. Slime touch gestures do not scroll the page.

Graphics use ordinary namespaced local scripts, without inline code or eval. Four scenes tested with script-src self. Bundle approximately 780KB before transfer compression. Rendering capped at roughly 20fps and pixel ratio 1.6; one scene at a time; hidden tabs pause. Reduced-motion mode disables idle motion. SVG gameplay fallback remains available without WebGL.

Phone/tablet viewport checks and offline recipe checks passed in desktop Chrome. Physical Android GPU performance, installation, microphone and pen/palm behaviour remain unverified. Native Hebrew diction remains unjudged.
