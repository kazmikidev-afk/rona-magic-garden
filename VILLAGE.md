# Rona's rainbow garden
An original short, age-five-oriented princess and unicorn game. Three guided tasks: water three flowers, collect three crown stars, then match three shapes to complete a rainbow bridge. No timer, losing, shop, ads, chat, account or child-data collection. Progress resets on exit/reload. All instructions have bundled free synthetic Hebrew audio; chime sounds respect mute. Repeat instruction and replay controls included. Touch targets at least 64px. Works offline once the app confirms its cache is ready. Existing Android add-to-home-screen button retained.

Original procedural stylized WebGL castle, princess, unicorn, flowers, stars and bridge. SVG scene fallback if WebGL is unavailable. Reduced-motion preference disables idle movement. Single scene with resources disposed on exit, capped pixel ratio 1.4 and frame rate about 20fps. No external runtime assets or services.

Checked in desktop Chrome at 390 and 1024 CSS px: all tasks and wrong-shape recovery, no overflow, all five voice clips decode, offline reload. Physical OnePlus GPU performance, installation and native Hebrew diction are not yet verified. This is a brief first adventure, not a large open-world game or photorealistic graphics.

## Chaptered adventure round (7 November 2026)
The garden is now a three-chapter adventure with rising difficulty, per Miki's request (more challenging, clearer, with continuity and better animations). Chapter map with unlock progression (session-only, in-memory; still no stored child data): chapter 1 "מעירים את הגן" (original difficulty), chapter 2 "פרחים חדשים", chapter 3 "הקשת הגדולה". Five missions per chapter: watering (3 taps; separate drops in ch2, shuffling drops in ch3), star collecting (3 stars; bobbing buttons in ch2, shuffling in ch3), bridge shapes (3/4/5 rounds, 3→4 options incl. hexagon, rotated options in ch3), counting (3/5/7 stars, numeric choices 2-4/4-6/6-8), name letter (ר, then נ, then ה among confusables ה/ח/ת).

Continuity inside the session: crown stars accumulate on the princess crown, rainbow arcs reveal per completed chapter (3/5/7), butterflies appear after chapter 1, a sparkle pond after chapter 2, and chapter cards stay marked complete. Clarity: visible chapter tag (פרק N מתוך 3) above the mission progress pills, explicit counts (X מתוך 3, צורה N מתוך M), hint glow on the correct choice after two misses. Animations: falling water drop, stars flying to the crown, bridge planks dropping in, growing flowers, orbiting butterflies, confetti on chapter completion; all reduced-motion aware.

Audio: existing bundled clips reused unchanged (v_water/v_stars/v_bridge/v_number generic across chapters; find_4/find_5 for the new letters; v_one..v_three counting). Four short new lines (chapter map, chapter 2/3 intros, final completion) recorded with the free Hila voice at build time as vn_* clips (village-voice-18.js) — a different voice from Achernar, pending Miki's/native-listener approval; cheap to re-record in Achernar later.
